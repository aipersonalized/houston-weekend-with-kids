# Local Discovery and Weather

### When to Load

Read in full before live discovery and weather assessment in the Houston planning procedure. This reference owns those checks; the saved attraction corpus is background, not the search boundary.

### Quick Reference

Search current neighborhood/community activities before settling on an itinerary. Verify selected leads with their organizers. Retrieve official forecasts and alerts before choosing outdoor blocks or weather-sensitive tickets. Show what the evidence changes and keep unavailable checks visible.

## Detailed Content

### Live local discovery

Use the host's available public-web search/browsing tools. The fixed-source collector does not search events or retrieve forecasts. Search with Houston, a public neighborhood or venue area, children's non-sensitive interest/admission categories and activity terms. Use deliberately supplied dates only when the caller's policy permits their use in public queries. Never send protected dates, origin, lodging or access details to search engines; protected matching requires a qualified bounded local adapter.

Use multiple source perspectives: current organizer/community calendars and an independent resident-oriented channel, such as a local parenting publication, neighborhood newsletter or public resident discussion. Vary the query by interest and place instead of repeatedly searching "top Houston attractions." Examples include Houston family nature programs, neighborhood children's art workshops, public library STEM programs, and community market family activities. These are query patterns, not preselected recommendations.

Live starting points checked on 2026-10-18:

| Source | What to inspect |
|---|---|
| [Houston Public Library calendar](https://calendar.houstonlibrary.org/calendar/events) | Branch, age/category, exact event page, occurrence year, registration and cancellation |
| [Houston Parks and Recreation](https://www.houstontx.gov/parks/index.html) | Community-center/park programs, current event links, repair/closure notices and public-access rules |
| [Levy Park calendar](https://www.levyparkhouston.org/calendar/) | Exact family program, recurrence exceptions, facility notices and weather conditions |
| [City neighborhood events](https://houstontx.gov/specialevents/sunday-in-the-park.html) | Current season/year and organizer confirmation, rather than assuming a recurring title means every Sunday |

These links are examples of source types, not an exhaustive venue list or a current event recommendation. Search other neighborhoods, independent arts/food/cultural organizations, nature centers and small businesses when they better match the family's interests. Nearby suburbs require explicit travel-time fit; "Houston area" does not mean nearby.

For each shortlisted local lead, retain discovery source, organizer source, activity/area, fit to interests/categories, current occurrence, total costs, entry/registration restrictions and weather exposure. A free program can require registration, residency, a library card or capacity checks; do not invent eligibility. Verify actual operating status and posted year, and reject expired/cancelled occurrences. An unofficial lead may remain an unverified suggestion, never a confirmed itinerary slot.

Carry suitable local and non-pass choices into the real comparison. Explain why a recommended choice fits; if a famous attraction fits better, it can still win. Do not manufacture obscurity, claim exclusive local knowledge, impose an arbitrary local-stop quota or add unwanted activities merely to appear varied.

### Weather decisions

Start with [NWS Houston/Galveston](https://www.weather.gov/hgx/) for official hazards/outlooks and the point or hourly forecast for each relevant public venue area. [The downtown Houston forecast](https://forecast.weather.gov/MapClick.php?lat=29.7604&lon=-95.3698) is a public city reference, not a household location or a substitute for a southeast/coastal venue forecast. The NWS page links hourly forecasts and area alerts. Open the actual forecast and alerts; a search snippet or a successful local corpus check is not a completed weather check.

Record retrieval time in Houston's timezone, source update/issue time when available, location/area, explicit forecast-valid periods, relevant conditions and alerts, and coverage gaps. Inspect temperature/heat information, rain timing, thunderstorms, wind and any flood/tropical or other official hazard that affects the chosen activities. Use available hourly detail for outdoor visits, walks, queues and travel. Do not claim a heat index or a no-alert result unless the source actually establishes it.

| Situation | Required handling |
|---|---|
| Permitted date-fit evidence matches the forecast window | Assess each day's exposed blocks and specify which stay, move, shorten or switch indoors, with a source-based reason |
| Dates unknown or private date matching unavailable | Retrieve the current public forecast/alerts, label them as current-area context only, and state that trip-weather fit is unknown; offer conditional indoor/outdoor plans |
| Intended dates beyond published coverage | State the forecast's actual end date/period and that trip conditions are unavailable; use labeled contingencies and specify a later recheck when dates enter coverage |
| Forecast or alert retrieval fails | Try another official public view when available, disclose the remaining check gap and keep recommendations conditional; never infer favorable weather |
| Conditions or operator status changes | Reassess exposed blocks, verify the replacement and send changed paid use/refund needs back to shopping |

Do not substitute today's observations, seasonal averages or a climate outlook for the trip forecast. A daily rain probability does not establish rain duration. For warnings or cancellations affecting a selected activity, follow official and operator guidance; an indoor venue does not resolve unsafe travel. Distinguish a weather risk from a confirmed closure.

Show the planning consequence even when weather is favorable: for example, an outdoor block remains suitable under the checked forecast, subject to refresh. Indoor replacements need their own occurrence, hours, age fit, capacity and travel checks. Recheck before a separately authorized weather-sensitive purchase and near departure; do not create a reminder or recurring job without a request.

## Key Concepts

- **Discovery lead:** A candidate found through local knowledge or a calendar, awaiting current organizer verification.
- **Weather coverage:** The area and periods a retrieved forecast actually describes.
- **Weather consequence:** A reasoned effect on activity selection, timing, travel, fallback or ticket flexibility.

## Decision Criteria

An itinerary is supported only when selected activity evidence and weather coverage fit its claimed status. Unknown timing, stale events, unavailable forecasts or unsupported private matching permit useful provisional options, not confirmed conditions or a final weather-qualified itinerary.

## Constraints & Limits

This procedure supplies instructions, not a new search engine, live weather adapter or private-date processor. Live sources can fail, show stale content or omit the relevant occurrence. Follow `references/privacy-and-capabilities.md` and any stricter user/host privacy or browser-session policy. Do not promise live discovery or weather verification when the host lacks the required public-web capability.

## Common Patterns

A local workshop and nearby park can form a day when their timing and weather fit. If rain or heat removes the park, verify an indoor substitute and reprice only affected admissions. If a local event replaces a paid pass stop, compare the remaining paid basket against no-pass admission before retaining the pass recommendation.
