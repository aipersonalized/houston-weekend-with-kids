### Geography and transit

[Visit Houston's family guide](https://www.visithoustontexas.com/things-to-do/attractions/things-to-do-with-kids/) places the Museum District and Space Center in distinct areas. Open its "Best Museums" section for background discovery. Its generic drive-time claims are not route evidence.

[METRO's Museum District guide](https://www.ridemetro.org/riding-metro/houston-attractions/museum-district), "Transit Services" and "Ride METRO", identifies the Red Line and Museum District station. Open those official sections when transit matters. Include station walks, waits, transfers and current alerts; a rail connection does not establish the full route.

Planning judgment: grouping each day around one main area generally reduces movement and scheduling risk. Test this against family priorities rather than treating it as a fixed attraction limit.

### Space Center Houston

[Plan Your Visit](https://spacecenter.org/visitor-information/guide/), "Choose your mission duration", offers 2-hour, 4-hour and 7+ hour models in the reviewed background. Open that official section to choose visit depth; a short block does not cover every experience. [Visitor Information](https://spacecenter.org/visitor-information/), "Parking & Map", "What You Can Bring" and FAQ, supplies entry/security, weather and arrival considerations. Confirm the current version of the relevant section.

[NASA Tram Tours](https://spacecenter.org/exhibits-and-experiences/nasa-tram-tour/), boarding sections, "Know Before You Go" and FAQ, distinguishes paid advance Mission Control from included first-come tours in the reviewed source. That snapshot gives 60–90 minute tours and last departure two hours before closing. Advance booking, on-site boarding, limited restrooms, stroller restrictions, open-air weather and possible NASA closures affect fit. Open those current official sections for the chosen tour; admission alone does not guarantee capacity.

### Museum District choices

[Children's Museum Houston](https://www.cmhouston.org/plan-a-visit), "Hours", "Admission" and "Top FAQ", encourages advance reservations and requires a child in the group in the reviewed source. Its observed free family night is Thursday, not a general weekend discount. Food/access policies can affect breaks. Open the relevant current official sections; assess interests without assuming ages or ability.

[HMNS Hours & Admission](https://www.hmns.org/visit/hours-admission/), "Regular Hours", "Special Attractions" and "Admission", states an average visit of two or more hours and separate attraction/show cutoffs in the reviewed source. Permanent halls and attractions such as the Butterfly Center or planetarium have different admission/time requirements. Open those current official sections and check chosen attraction ticket/entry details before scheduling.

[Houston Zoo hours](https://www.houstonzoo.org/plan-your-visit/hours/), "Daytime Admission Hours", requires nonmember online timed reservations and distinguishes last entry from closing in the reviewed source. Open that current official section; confirm pass-channel instructions through comparison. Include outdoor-weather/walking allowances. Seasonal evening events can have separate admission.

## Key Concepts

- **Anchor:** Priority visit setting the day's area and time budget.
- **Visit depth:** Specific experiences covered by a dwell-time estimate.
- **Entry dependency:** Required ticket, reservation, boarding pass or add-on.
- **Weather swap:** Replacement with its own admission/schedule check.
- **Provisional:** Useful planning conditional on missing inputs/evidence.

## Decision Criteria

| Condition | Planning response |
|---|---|
| A neighborhood program, community event or small venue fits the family | Verify its current organizer listing and compare it fairly with pass and free alternatives |
| Weather check is missing, unmatched or outside forecast coverage | Keep the plan conditional, state the exact gap and retain verified alternatives |
| Space/NASA is a priority | Choose tour/exhibits and appropriate duration first |
| Two museums are desired | Check interests, admission products, dwell/movement and breaks before combining |
| Outdoor weather or walking matters | Offer a checked replacement or lighter day; preserve private access details |
| Pass includes more than desired or usable | Send only the intended feasible set to shopping |
| Dates/categories/adults are unknown | Offer conditional patterns and minimum questions; withhold totals/capacity claims |

## Constraints & Limits

This is a small public collection, not an exhaustive Houston guide. A new venue needs current official evidence. No account, exact-address, private-record or reservation adapter is supplied. Local integrity checks establish file consistency; source fidelity and applicability require separate review.

## Common Patterns

- **Museum-focused draft:** Priority museum, meal/rest, optional nearby museum or park if weather, time and pace permit. Treat the second venue as a swap when tight.
- **Space-focused draft:** Match a published visit model, allow arrival/security/travel and honor selected tram dependencies. Keep distant evening visits optional.
- **Local-discovery draft:** A currently verified neighborhood program or independent venue plus a nearby complementary activity, chosen from live research and weather fit.
- **Unknown-date request:** Search current local options and inspect available public forecast/alerts, but label event-date and trip-weather fit unknown; avoid final totals or live-capacity claims.
